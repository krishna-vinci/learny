# M14 — Plan visuals and videos per chapter, and use source images

**Goal:** every chapter is planned with its visuals and at least one high-quality video, the plan is refined once sources are in (figures, data tables, transcripts make visuals easy and accurate), and figures from sources are kept locally with credit so agents can use or redraw them.

## Fixed decisions
- **E1 Two-stage media planning.** Stage 1 (planning): the outliner writes, per curriculum chapter, a short media intent: 1–2 visuals (form: figure/diagram, chart, widget type, sketch/story; the concept each explains) and the video need (concept/moment a video should show). Stage 2 (after sources settle — kickoff completion, and again in pre-draft preflight): a per-chapter **media brief** is refined from what the sources actually offer: candidate source figures (local, credited), data tables usable for charts/widgets, and ≥1 chosen video with a transcript moment. Store both as plain files in the study tree (implementer chooses the shape — e.g. `Media:` lines in `curriculum.md` + `<set>/media/NN-slug.md` briefs — and documents it in `docs/STUDY_TREE.md`). Keep `curriculum.md` parsing backward compatible.
- **E2 At least one high-quality video per chapter.** Per chapter, scout with the M13 video routing using the chapter's concepts; quality bar: named educator/institution or well-known education channel, watch URL, length fit (prefer 3–25 min or a moment range), level fit, English unless the plan's language differs; rank (classifier `video.useful`/`sources.rank` or LLM fallback). Ingest via the transcript ladder. If nothing passes the bar, record "no suitable video: <reason>" in the brief — never add a weak video to fill the quota. Videos without transcripts are watch-only (D34) but still count as the chapter's video when quality is high.
- **E3 Source figures kept locally with credit.** At ingest (and on Refresh), download the most relevant figures of a source (caption/heading match to the source's sections; skip logos, icons, ads, tracking pixels; ≤ 12 per source, size/type limits as `save_asset`; SSRF-safe fetch) into `library/<id>/figures/` and rewrite `images.json` entries to `{path, url, alt, caption, section, license?, credit}`. Detect licenses where the page or host states them (Wikimedia Commons, OpenStax/LibreTexts CC BY, etc.).
- **E4 Reuse rule (copyright):** chapters may embed a source figure directly only when its license allows reuse (CC BY/BY-SA/public domain) — with credit in the caption; otherwise the agent **redraws** it as an SVG figure/widget (citing the source) instead of copying. Encode in `make-visual`/`media-authoring` and enforce by a write-time warning when a non-permissive figure path is embedded.
- **E5 Drafter gets the brief.** The draft (and rewrite) task includes the chapter's media brief: planned visuals with their source data/figures (paths + captions + license), the chosen video with its moment, and the instruction to realise each planned visual (or explain in one muted line why not). Visuals built from source data/tables cite them.
- **E6 Visible.** Course plan rows and the Plan page show per chapter: planned visuals (made / planned) and video (chosen / none-with-reason). No new screens beyond these.
