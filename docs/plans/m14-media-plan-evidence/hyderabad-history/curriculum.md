# Curriculum

- [x] 01 — Hyderabad on the Map and Timeline
  Scope: Locate the Deccan Plateau, Musi River, Golconda, Old City, Secunderabad, and modern metropolis, separating city and state.
  Prerequisites: none
  Visual: figure — Labelled regional and city map with scale and north arrow
  Visual: chart — Political eras and surviving landmarks on one timeline
  Video: A narrated map-to-street orientation linking the river and urban centres
- [x] 02 — Before Hyderabad: Deccan and Golconda
  Scope: Survey Kakatiya and Bahmani layers and Golconda's emergence as a fortress and trading centre.
  Prerequisites: 01
  Visual: diagram — Bahmani fragmentation and Golconda's regional connections
  Visual: sketch — Hilltop defence and settlement around Golconda
  Video: An expert site walkthrough showing terrain, gates, and trade routes
- [x] 03 — Qutb Shahis and the Founding of Hyderabad
  Scope: Explore Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah, the 1591 foundation, Charminar, trade, and cultural exchange while distinguishing evidence from founding legends.
  Prerequisites: 01, 02
  Visual: diagram — Charminar and the intersecting streets of the planned city
  Visual: chart — Documented events alongside attributed traditions
  Video: A historian reading the city plan and explaining why a new capital mattered
- [x] 04 — Reading the Qutb Shahi City in Stone
  Scope: Interpret Golconda Fort, Qutb Shahi Tombs, Charminar, Mecca Masjid, and waterworks through plans and visible architectural features.
  Prerequisites: 03
  Visual: figure — Annotated monument photographs identifying arches, domes, and materials
  Visual: diagram — Stepwell and water-storage relationships
  Video: A conservation specialist demonstrating stone, lime plaster, and water structures on site
- [ ] 05 — Mughal Conquest and the Rise of the Nizams
  Scope: Follow the 1687 conquest, Mughal administration, and Asaf Jahi emergence in the wider Deccan.
  Prerequisites: 02, 03
  Visual: chart — 1687–1724 transition with the changing meaning of authority
  Visual: figure — Deccan map relating Mughal, Maratha, and Asaf Jahi power
  Video: A historian explaining conquest and provincial autonomy using a map
- [x] 06 — Hyderabad State and British Paramountcy
  Scope: Understand Nizam autonomy, British alliances, the Residency, Secunderabad cantonment, administration, and nineteenth-century reform.
  Prerequisites: 05
  Visual: diagram — Nizam, Resident, and cantonment as overlapping authorities
  Visual: figure — Residency and Secunderabad on a dated city map
  Video: An expert explaining the Residency's architecture and political role
- [x] 07 — Making a Modern Capital
  Scope: Connect railways, education, public institutions, the 1908 Musi flood, reservoirs, and late-Nizam planning to urban life.
  Prerequisites: 06
  Visual: diagram — Musi, upstream reservoirs, and flood-response infrastructure
  Visual: figure — Before-and-after streetscapes and civic institutions
  Video: Anuradha Naik's explanation of early-twentieth-century architecture and city life
- [ ] 08 — 1947–1948: Accession, Conflict, and Memory
  Scope: Examine the standstill agreement, Razakars, Telangana rebellion, Operation Polo, civilian suffering, and differences between attributed accounts.
  Prerequisites: 06
  Visual: chart — Parallel diplomatic, military, and peasant-struggle timelines
  Visual: diagram — Source-comparison matrix separating events, estimates, and interpretations
  Video: Contextualised historical footage with an expert distinguishing military events from civilian experiences
- [ ] 09 — From Hyderabad State to Telangana
  Scope: Trace democratic government, the 1956 linguistic reorganisation, Andhra Pradesh's capital, metropolitan growth, the Telangana movement, and 2014 statehood.
  Prerequisites: 08
  Visual: figure — Boundary maps for Hyderabad State, 1956 reorganisation, and 2014 Telangana
  Visual: chart — Political transitions alongside industry and metropolitan growth
  Video: An accessible expert map explanation of 1956 and 2014 without partisan simplification
- [ ] 10 — A Living Deccani Culture
  Scope: Relate Dakhni/Urdu and Telugu, religions, festivals, literature, music, clothing, crafts, cuisine, bazaars, and etiquette to exchange and community diversity.
  Prerequisites: 03, 06
  Visual: diagram — Languages, cultural institutions, and neighbourhood connections without equating religion with language
  Visual: story — A market visit connecting craft, food, and respectful interaction
  Video: A specialist interpreting poetry or musical objects alongside a subtitled example of living culture
- [ ] 11 — Heritage, Development, and Competing Memories
  Scope: Compare restored and lost places, urban pressures, naming and tourism narratives, and official, scholarly, and community perspectives.
  Prerequisites: 04, 08, 09, 10
  Visual: figure — AKDN restoration photographs paired with a dated historic view
  Visual: chart — Different stakeholders' claims and the evidence needed to assess them
  Video: A conservation architect explaining how heritage choices affect city life
- [ ] 12 — Build a History-Led Tourist Itinerary
  Scope: Design Golconda/Qutb Shahi and Old City/Nizam-era heritage days with monument-reading prompts, food stops, transport, respectful conduct, and current access checks.
  Prerequisites: 04, 07, 08, 10, 11
  Visual: figure — Two route maps distinguishing walking segments from transport transfers
  Visual: chart — Time, rest, heat, accessibility, worship schedules, and alternative stops
  Video: A qualified heritage guide modelling observation, visitor etiquette, and realistic movement between stops
