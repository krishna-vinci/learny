## Goal
Build an introductory but substantial understanding of Hyderabad's history and living legacy, and use it to explore the city as an informed, respectful tourist.

## Scope — in
A visual timeline; Deccan setting; Golconda and the Qutb Shahis; Hyderabad's founding; Mughal conquest; the Nizams and British relationship; 1948 integration; post-independence change; architecture, languages, religions, food, markets, water, and practical heritage itineraries.

## Scope — out
Exhaustive dynastic genealogy, specialist historiography, archaeological technique, detailed contemporary party politics, and a comprehensive guide to every attraction in the metropolitan region.

## Pace and approach
About four hours weekly across 12 weeks, without a deadline: two short evening sessions for photographs, reading and interactive examples, plus a weekend synthesis. Begin with a real Charminar photograph, separate visible observations from historical inference, then build the map and chronology. No mathematical or proof prerequisites; the profile's Python/calculus background does not require coding here. Use free, self-hostable interactive attachments and downloadable reading; paid tours and subscriptions are unnecessary.

Each unit ends with a small artifact: annotated photograph, timeline, source comparison, or itinerary stop card. Success means explaining the main transitions without confusing Hyderabad city with Hyderabad State, interpreting six monuments, comparing two accounts of 1948, and designing two respectful heritage days.

## Reading strategy
Reuse the nine selected sources. Wikipedia supplies orientation; UNESCO submissions supply monument descriptions, not proof of World Heritage inscription; Incredible India supplies tourist context, not critical historiography. AKDN describes its own conservation work. Cambridge's princely-cities introduction supplies an optional expert lens in plain language. LSE currently contains an abstract and PDF link only; Beyond colonial urbanism contains an abstract and references, not its paywalled argument. Do not teach unsupported detailed conclusions from either landing page. Obtain accessible full text before detailed use. No new research papers are required for this introductory course.
