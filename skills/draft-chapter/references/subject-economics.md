# Teaching economics

## What a great chapter does

Economics studies choices under constraints and their consequences. Begin with
an everyday tradeoff, then separate a model's assumptions from observed data.
The learner should explain a mechanism, use a simple worked example and say
what evidence could challenge the explanation.

## Skeleton

1. **A tradeoff** (2–4 sentences): a concrete choice with costs and benefits.
2. **The question and vocabulary:** introduce only the terms needed for the mechanism.
3. **A model:** state assumptions, units, time horizon and what is held constant.
4. **Worked example:** show the reasoning or calculation, then change one assumption.
5. **Evidence:** use dated official central-bank, IMF/World Bank or statistics-office
   data; specify geography, units, series definition and revisions where relevant.
6. **Competing explanations:** where contested, present the major schools' strongest
   arguments with attribution; separate positive claims from normative judgments.
7. **Limits and policy tradeoffs:** explain uncertainty and distributional effects;
   never turn a simplified model into a guaranteed forecast or political verdict.
8. Check yourself → Key takeaways → bridge.

## Blocks

`:::definition` for terms; `:::example` for a calculation or a model applied to a
case; `:::deeper` for identification, measurement or a contested assumption.
Use open textbooks for foundations. Charts need dates, sources, units and clear
labels; do not confuse nominal and real amounts, stocks and flows or levels and
rates of change.

## Pitfalls

Correlation as causation; undated indicators; unsupported forecasts; treating
one school's assumptions as universal truth; a political or moral judgment
stated as fact; ignoring who gains and loses. Attribute interpretations and
explain evidence strength without suggesting every explanation is equally supported.

> You have one hour and two useful tasks. Choosing the first means giving up
> what the second might have produced. That forgone possibility is part of the
