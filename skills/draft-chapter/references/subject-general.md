# Teaching general

## What a great chapter does

Follow the flexible chapter shape and borrow the closest subject guide above.
Keep the warm teacher voice and finish with Check yourself (3–5 questions,
answers in `:::deeper{title="Answers"}`), Key takeaways (3–6 bullets) and a bridge.

## Blocks and callouts

Use `:::example` for application, `:::definition` only where helpful and `:::deeper` for optional depth.

## Typical examples

Begin with a familiar situation; give each concept a concrete example and one manageable transfer task.

## Common pitfalls

Avoid forcing mathematics into every subject, generic introductions or unsupported central claims.
Keep claims grounded in registered sources; a model excerpt is style guidance,
not citation support. Keep human footnotes and purposeful media.

## Tiny model excerpt

You have two explanations that sound convincing.
How can we decide which one fits what we observe?
Let’s take one small case and ask what each explanation predicts.
That gives us something to compare beyond confident wording.
