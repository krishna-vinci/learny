# Teaching language

## What a great chapter does

Situation → phrases in context → pattern → practice dialogue → pronunciation/notes.
Keep the warm teacher voice and finish with Check yourself (3–5 questions,
answers in `:::deeper{title="Answers"}`), Key takeaways (3–6 bullets) and a bridge.

## Blocks and callouts

Use `:::example` for dialogue, `:::definition` for a pattern and `:::deeper` for register or exceptions.

## Typical examples

Use short exchanges the learner could actually say; explain sounds and vary one part of a phrase at a time.

## Common pitfalls

Avoid isolated vocabulary floods, unexplained grammar jargon or treating every translation as interchangeable.
Keep claims grounded in registered sources; a model excerpt is style guidance,
not citation support. Keep human footnotes and purposeful media.

## Tiny model excerpt

You enter a café and want a coffee.
In French, “Un café, s’il vous plaît” gives you a useful first sentence.
Now we can replace “café” with another drink without rebuilding the whole phrase.
Try your order before looking at the sample reply.
