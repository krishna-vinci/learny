# Teaching practical

## What a great chapter does

Goal → steps → common mistakes → safety → practice routine.
Keep the warm teacher voice and finish with Check yourself (3–5 questions,
answers in `:::deeper{title="Answers"}`), Key takeaways (3–6 bullets) and a bridge.

## Blocks and callouts

Use `:::example` for a demonstration, `:::definition` for essential terms and `:::deeper` for advanced variants.

## Typical examples

State needed materials, show an achievable task, include checkpoints and a short repeatable routine.

## Common pitfalls

Do not bury hazards, skip prerequisites or promise results; scale practice and safety to the task.
Keep claims grounded in registered sources; a model excerpt is style guidance,
not citation support. Keep human footnotes and purposeful media.

## Tiny model excerpt

You want a clean, even stitch rather than a fast seam.
Start on a scrap of fabric so mistakes cost little.
We’ll sew a short line, check the tension, then repeat.
If the fabric puckers, pause and adjust before continuing.
